⚡ 2. Technology Stack & Architectural Rationale

Every technology in SevaSathi was selected intentionally to maximize accessibility, speed on low-end mobile devices, and developer agility during high-stress deployments.

Frontend: Vanilla HTML5 / CSS3 / ES6+ Zero Overhead

  • Zero Compilation Lag: Gig workers operate – budget smartphones on 3G/4G networks. Avoids 3MB–5MB React/Angular virtual-DOM bundle bloat.
  • Native Web Speech API: Direct, zero-latency access to device microphone speech recognition for voice booking.
  • MutationObserver i18n: Live trilingual translation engine without framework hydration race conditions.

Backend: Node.js & Express.js High Concurrency

  • Non-Blocking Event Loop: Manages thousands of concurrent chat rooms, live bids, and booking status updates efficiently.
  • Unified JS Language Stack: Seamless data structures shared between frontend validation and backend controllers.
  • Lightweight REST Middleware: Robust authentication, rate-limiting, and dispute routing.

Database: MongoDB (NoSQL) Schema Flexibility

  • Dynamic Schemas: A plumber's profile (tools, licenses) differs from an enterprise contract. MongoDB stores both without rigid SQL migrations.
  • Nested JSON Subdocuments: Real-time bargaining iterations ([{offeredBy: 'customer', rate: 400}, {counter: 450}]) stored naturally inside single booking documents.
  • High Write Throughput: Fast ingestion for high-frequency community chat messages.

Data Format: Native JSON Universal Standard

  • Zero Serialization Friction: JSON.parse() and JSON.stringify() run natively in all browsers and engines without ORM mismatches.
  • Modular Locale Files: /locales/hi.json and /locales/bn.json allow zero-downtime updates to translation dictionaries.
  • Human-Readable Payloads: Simplifies debugging and transparent API audit trails.

BUSINESS Q1: How is SevaSathi different from Urban Company or TaskRabbit?
Answer: Traditional apps operate as rent-seeking middlemen extracting 25–40% cuts with opaque algorithms and zero safety nets. SevaSathi is built as a democratic worker cooperative: workers retain 85% of revenue, 10% funds their collective insurance/pension pool, and 5% covers platform servers. We also provide AI voice booking for non-literate workers.
FINANCIAL Q2: If you only take a 5% platform fee, how is the business model sustainable?
Answer: We eliminate corporate overhead, physical call centers (automated with our AI assistant), and high acquisition bloat. In addition to the 5% fee, we monetize B2B Enterprise workforce contracts (hotels, facility management) through high-volume corporate subscription tiers.
TECH STACK Q3: Why didn't you build the frontend in React, Next.js, or Vue?
Answer: Our frontline workers use budget Android phones (– devices) on patchy 3G/4G networks. React's virtual-DOM hydration introduces perceptible lag and memory bloat. Vanilla ES6+ guarantees 100/100 Lighthouse performance, instant load times, zero build failure risk, and direct access to native browser Speech APIs.
AI & NLP Q4: How does your AI Assistant handle mixed colloquial Indic languages (Hinglish / Banglish)?
Answer: Our AI pipeline combines rule-based heuristic symptom normalizers with Gemini LLM fallbacks. It understands colloquial phrases (e.g., "pani tapak raha hai" or "fan cholche na"), mapping them to standardized trade categories and extracting Indic numerals and relative dates ("kal subah", "porshu").
SECURITY Q5: How do you prevent fraud, fake workers, and customer payment defaults?
Answer: We enforce a 3-layer security system: (1) Pre-funded Escrow locks payment before worker travels; (2) Worker document verification with QR ID verification; (3) Customer and worker phone numbers remain masked until mutual confirmation; (4) 3-strike warning ledger permanently bans abusive actors.
