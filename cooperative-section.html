📞 Voice IVR & Dual SMS Dispatch Model

Enabling zero-internet, feature-phone accessibility for all frontline workers. Workers simply call the SevaSathi toll-free helpline, hear live available jobs near them, book directly using their phone dial-pad, select date and time over the call, and instant SMS confirmations are automatically dispatched to both worker and customer.

Helpline / Dial Access 1800-SEVASATHI (Toll-Free 24x7 Multi-Lingual)

🔄 Complete Call & Message Workflow Architecture

How the automated voice and message dispatch engine connects workers and customers without requiring smartphones, apps, or mobile internet:

1
📞 Inbound Worker Call

A registered worker simply dials the SevaSathi toll-free helpline. The AI IVR identifies their trade, location, and preferred language (English, हिन्दी, বাংলা).

2
🔢 Dial-Pad Job Selection

The system reads out nearby open jobs with rates and localities. The worker selects their desired job by pressing the matching number on their dial-pad (e.g. Press 1, 2, or 3).

3
⏱️ Direct Schedule Confirmation

The worker selects their available service date and time slot right on the call via key-press or voice input. The job is instantly locked and assigned.

4
📩 Dual SMS Confirmation

Instant booking confirmation messages are simultaneously dispatched via SMS/WhatsApp to both the Worker and the Customer with contact details, address, and verification OTP.
